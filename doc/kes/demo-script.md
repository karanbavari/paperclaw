# Demo Script — Structured Demo Agenda & Talking Points

**Owner:** Sales-Agent (KES)
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-15

---

## Overview

This document defines the standard demo flow for qualified leads. The demo is structured as a **discovery-rich conversation**, not a feature walkthrough. Every demo must map back to the prospect's stated pain points and desired outcomes.

**Entry trigger:** Lead has been qualified (BANT or equivalent) and agreed to a demo.

**Exit criteria:** Prospect has seen relevant value, next steps are defined, and a proposal path is agreed or proposed.

---

## Pre-Demo Preparation Checklist

Complete this checklist at least 2 hours before the demo:

- [ ] Review CRM record — lead source, company, title, industry
- [ ] Review intent signals — content downloaded, pages visited, events attended
- [ ] Review previous email/call notes — stated pain points, objections, questions
- [ ] Identify 2-3 specific use cases to demonstrate
- [ ] Prepare 1-2 relevant customer stories / case studies
- [ ] Check for decision-maker presence on the call
- [ ] Confirm technical requirements (screen share, guest access, integrations)
- [ ] Set up demo environment with prospect-relevant data (use mock data matching their industry)
- [ ] Prepare agenda and send calendar invite with Zoom/calendly link

**CRM fields to verify before demo:**

| Field | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Company name & size | Tailor scale-related examples |
| Industry | Use relevant vertical use cases |
| Pain points (from discovery) | Primary demo focus |
| Decision-maker(s) on call | Adjust depth and Q&A accordingly |
| Current solution / competitor | Positioning and comparison points |
| Timeline to decision | Urgency and next-step cadence |
| Budget range (if known) | Proposal scope |

## Demo Structure (60-Minute Standard)

### 0:00-5:00 — Opening & Agenda

**Purpose:** Set expectations. Establish rapport. Confirm discovery context.

**Script:**

"Thanks for the time today, [First Name]. Here's what I'd like to cover:

1. **Confirm what you're hoping to solve** (5 min) — make sure we're focused on the right things
2. **Show how [Company Name] addresses that** (30 min) — tailored to what you've shared
3. **Quick example from a similar customer** (10 min)
4. **Next steps** (5 min) — if it makes sense, we'll define the path forward

How does that sound?"

**Check:**
- Confirm decision-maker presence ("Who else should be part of this conversation?")
- Confirm time available ("We have 60 minutes — does that still work for everyone?")
- Re-state pain points ("From our conversation, I understand [X, Y, Z] are the key priorities — does that capture it?")

---

### 5:00-10:00 — Discovery Deepening

**Purpose:** Uncover additional context. Validate our understanding. Identify evaluation criteria.

**Key questions to ask (select 2-3 based on prior context):**

1. "Walk me through how you're currently handling [Pain Point] — what's working, what's not?"
2. "What does success look like for you in [Timeframe]? How will you measure it?"
3. "Have you evaluated other solutions? What's important to you in the decision?"
4. "Who else is involved in the evaluation, and what matters to them?"
5. "If this demo goes well, what would need to happen for you to move forward?"

**Do NOT:**
- Jump into the product before understanding context
- Assume you know the problem based on industry alone
- Pitch features they haven't expressed interest in

---

### 10:00-40:00 — Tailored Demo (Core)

**Purpose:** Show the solution addressing their specific pain points. Use their language. Connect features to outcomes.

**Structure by pain point:**

**For each pain point the prospect identified:**
1. **Name the pain:** "You mentioned [Pain Point] — let me show you how we address that."
2. **Show the relevant feature:** 2-3 minutes focused on the specific workflow
3. **Explain the "so what":** "What this means for you is [Outcome]."
4. **Verify resonance:** "Does this approach make sense for how you work?"

**Demo flow template:**

| Segment | Time | What to Show | Talking Point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overview | 5 min | Dashboard / Home screen | "This is what you'll see day-to-day. Focused on [Key Outcome]." |
| Pain Point 1 | 8 min | Feature addressing their #1 pain | "Let's look at how this solves [Specific Problem]." |
| Pain Point 2 | 7 min | Feature addressing their #2 pain | "And for [Second Pain] — here's the workflow." |
| Pain Point 3 | 5 min | Feature addressing their #3 pain (or integration) | "One more thing that [Similar Customer] found valuable." |
| Customer story | 5 min | Brief case study tie-in | "[Customer] had a similar challenge and saw [Result]." |

**Demo best practices:**
- Use the prospect's data / industry scenarios in the demo environment
- Do not show every feature — only what maps to their stated needs
- Pause every 5-7 minutes to check for questions
- Use the "show, don't tell" principle — let the product speak
- If a feature doesn't work, move on gracefully without apologizing

---

### 40:00-50:00 — Customer Proof Points

**Purpose:** Build credibility. Reduce perceived risk. Make outcomes tangible.

**Select the most relevant case study:**

"Before we wrap up, I want to share how **[Similar Customer]** solved a similar challenge.

**They were facing:**
[1-2 sentence summary matching prospect's situation]

**With [Company Name], they achieved:**
- **[Metric 1]:** [Result]
- **[Metric 2]:** [Result]

**What they said about working with us:**
"[Quote from customer]"

**How this relates to you:**
[1-2 sentence bridge to prospect's situation]

[Link to full case study if interested]

---

### 50:00-60:00 — Next Steps & Close

**Purpose:** Define clear path forward. Address remaining questions. Create urgency without pressure.

**Script:**

"[First Name], based on what we've covered today, it seems like [Company Name] could help with [1-2 stated priorities].

**Here's what I'd suggest for next steps:**
1. I'll send a **custom proposal** outlining [Solution Scope] and [Pricing/Options]
2. We can schedule a **technical deep-dive** with your team if needed
3. I'll connect you with **[Reference Customer]** if you'd like to hear their experience firsthand

**Timeline consideration:** [If applicable — e.g., "We're seeing best results when teams get started by [Date] to align with [Quarter/Budget Cycle]."]

**Questions before we wrap up?**

- What's the most important thing you'll need to see to feel confident moving forward?
- Is there anything unclear about how [Company Name] fits your needs?
- Who else needs to be involved in the decision?

**If positive:** "Great — I'll send the proposal by [Date/Time]. If you have questions in the meantime, my calendar is open: [Calendly Link]."

**If hesitant:** "What's giving you pause? I'd love to address that before we move forward."

**If negative / not a fit:** "I appreciate your honesty. If your situation changes, I'd be happy to revisit. In the meantime, here's [Resource] that may be useful regardless."

## Common Demo Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Technical Buyer (CTO / Engineer)

| Aspect | Approach |
|---|---|
| Focus | Architecture, integrations, API, security, performance |
| Avoid | High-level business value fluff |
| Emphasize | Documentation, developer experience, self-serve capabilities |
| Case study | Reference engineering team, not just business outcome |
| Next step | Technical evaluation (POC, sandbox, integration test) |

### Scenario 2: Business Buyer (VP / Director)

| Aspect | Approach |
|---|---|
| Focus | ROI, outcomes, team efficiency, competitive advantage |
| Avoid | Deep technical implementation details |
| Emphasize | Case studies with metrics, time-to-value, support |
| Case study | Reference business outcome, not technical specs |
| Next step | Proposal with pricing, executive summary |

### Scenario 3: End User / Team Lead

| Aspect | Approach |
|---|---|
| Focus | Day-to-day workflow, ease of use, time savings |
| Avoid | Pricing discussion, high-level strategy |
| Emphasize | UI walkthrough, quick wins, training/support resources |
| Case study | Reference a peer team's experience |
| Next step | Free trial or sandbox access, training session |

## Demo Fail-State Recovery

| Issue | Recovery Script |
|---|---|
| Feature doesn't work | "That's not behaving as expected — let me note that and show you an alternative path instead." Move on. Do not dwell. |
| Prospect goes silent | "Does that make sense so far? Happy to go deeper on anything or adjust direction." |
| Prospect asks about competitor pricing | "I'm not going to comment on [Competitor]'s pricing — I'll make sure our proposal is competitive and reflects the value you'll get. Fair?" |
| Prospect brings unexpected stakeholder | Pivot to appropriate persona depth. Offer follow-up for specific needs. |
| Technical question you can't answer | "Great question — let me note that and have our Solutions Engineer follow up. Is that OK?" |
| Prospect is distracted / multitasking | "I want to make sure this is valuable for you — would it be better to reschedule when you can focus?" |
| Negative body language / energy | "I'm sensing this might not be hitting the mark. What would be more useful to cover?" |

## Post-Demo Actions

### Within 2 Hours
- [ ] Send personalized thank-you email with:
  - Recap of key points discussed
  - Link to recorded demo (if applicable)
  - Case study or resource mentioned during demo
  - Calendar link for next meeting (if agreed)
  - Proposal delivery date commitment
- [ ] Log demo notes in CRM

### Within 24 Hours
- [ ] Update lead stage in CRM
- [ ] Add detailed notes: pain points confirmed, features shown, questions asked
- [ ] Tag any competitive intel gathered
- [ ] Schedule follow-up task based on agreed next steps

## Demo Success Criteria

| Metric | Target | Action if Missed |
|---|---|---|
| Next step defined | 100% of demos | Never end a demo without a clear next step |
| Proposal sent within 48h | > 90% | Internal process review |
| Demo-to-proposal conversion | > 60% | Review discovery quality |
| Proposal-to-close conversion | > 30% | Review pricing, positioning, or competitive landscape |
| Demo satisfaction score (if requested) | > 4/5 | Review demo quality and preparation |
