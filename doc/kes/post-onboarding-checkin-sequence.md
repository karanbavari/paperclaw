# Post-Onboarding Check-In Sequence Templates

**Owner:** Follow-up Specialist (KES)
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-14

---

## Overview

This document defines the 30/60/90 day check-in sequence for customers post-onboarding. Each touchpoint includes purpose, timing, template content, success criteria, and escalation rules for at-risk accounts.

**Handoff trigger:** Customer-Onboarding team marks onboarding complete in CRM.

---

## Tracked Fields in CRM

Every check-in touchpoint should log:
- Date sent
- Customer tier (T1/T2/T3)
- Response received (Y/N)
- Sentiment score (1-5)
- NPS score (when applicable)
- Risk flag (green/yellow/red)
- Follow-up action taken

---

## 30-Day Check-In (First Value Validation)

### Purpose

Validate the customer has achieved first value. Identify early friction. Reinforce key features and usage patterns. Begin relationship building.

### Timing

- Send: 28-32 days post-onboarding completion
- Best day: Tuesday or Wednesday
- Best time: 9:00-10:00 AM local time

### Template: 30-Day Check-In Email

**Subject:** How's everything going with [Product Name]?

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

It's been about a month since you started using [Product Name], and I wanted to check in personally.

**Quick question:** On a scale of 1-10, how confident are you that [Product Name] is helping you achieve [Primary Goal from onboarding]?

Here's what I'd love to learn from you:

1. **What's working well?** Are there features you're finding especially valuable?
2. **What's not working?** Any friction points or missing capabilities?
3. **Usage habits:** How often are you logging in? Weekly? Daily?
4. **Goals check:** Are you on track with the goals we set during onboarding?

**Want a quick optimization session?** I'm happy to hop on a 15-minute call to review how you're using the product and share tips to get even more value. Pick a time here: [Calendly Link]

Otherwise, just reply to this email — I read every response personally.

Best,
[Your Name]
Follow-up Specialist | [Company Name]

### Template: 30-Day Success Criteria

| Criteria | Target | Risk Flag |
|---|---|---|
| Customer reply received | Yes | Red if no reply after 7 days |
| Confidence score (1-10) | >= 7 | Yellow if 4-6, Red if < 4 |
| Active usage (logins/week) | >= 3 | Yellow if 1-2, Red if 0 |
| Primary goal on track | Yes | Red if "No" |
| NPS (if collected) | >= 7 | Yellow if 4-6, Red if < 4 |

### At-Risk Handoff Rules

| Risk Level | Action | Handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Log response, schedule 60-day check-in | None |
| Yellow | Send tip/training resources, flag in CRM for monitoring, schedule 60-day check-in with extra attention | Notify Customer-Onboarding team via CRM note |
| Red | Escalate immediately to CMO + Sales-Lead with full context | Create [KES-X] issue for CMO review |

### 30-Day Re-Engagement (if no reply after 7 days)

**Subject:** Didn't want to miss you — quick check-in

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Just a gentle nudge — I'd love to hear how your first month with [Product Name] has been. Your success is important to us, and your feedback helps us make the product better for you.

If everything is going great, just reply "All good!" and I'll check back in another month.

If there's anything that needs attention, I'm here to help — reply directly and I'll personally look into it.

Best,
[Your Name]
Follow-up Specialist | [Company Name]

## 60-Day Check-In (Value Deepening)

### Purpose

Deepen product adoption. Introduce advanced features. Identify expansion opportunities. Catch churn risk before it becomes entrenched.

### Timing

- Send: 58-62 days post-onboarding
- Best day: Tuesday or Wednesday
- Best time: 10:00-11:00 AM local time

### Template: 60-Day Check-In Email

**Subject:** 2 months in — what's next for you and [Product Name]?

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Two months in! You've got the fundamentals down. Now I want to help you level up.

**A few things to consider:**

**Feature Spotlight:** [Feature Name] — Did you know [Product Name] can also [capability that addresses a common need]? Customers who use this feature see [metric] improvement. [Link to docs/tutorial]

**Usage Insights:** Based on your account, you're using [X% of available features]. The highest-performing customers in your industry typically use [Y features]. Here's a quick comparison:

- ✅ [Feature used]
- ✅ [Feature used]
- ❌ [Feature not yet used — opportunity]
- ❌ [Feature not yet used — opportunity]

**Question for you:** Are there new goals or challenges on your horizon that [Product Name] could help with?

Also — if you or your team would benefit from a 30-minute advanced training session, let me know. Happy to set one up.

Reply to this email or book time here: [Calendly Link]

Best,
[Your Name]
Follow-up Specialist | [Company Name]

### Template: 60-Day Success Criteria

| Criteria | Target | Risk Flag |
|---|---|---|
| Customer reply received | Yes | Red if no reply after 5 days |
| Sentiment score (1-5) | >= 4 | Yellow if 3, Red if <= 2 |
| Feature adoption rate | >= 60% of core features | Yellow if 30-59%, Red if < 30% |
| Advanced training interest | Recorded | Info only |
| Expansion signal identified | Tracked if present | Info only |

### At-Risk Handoff Rules

| Risk Level | Action | Handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Log response, schedule 90-day check-in, note any feature interest for Sales-Lead | Pass expansion signals to Sales-Lead if present |
| Yellow | Schedule a call to address concerns, send relevant case studies, notify CMO | CMO notified via CRM note + weekly report |
| Red | Immediate escalation to CMO + Sales-Lead. Customer success intervention needed. | Create [KES-X] issue, assign to CMO for triage |

### 60-Day Re-Engagement (if no reply after 5 days)

**Subject:** Still thinking about you — a quick update

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

I know things get busy — just wanted to make sure you're getting the most out of [Product Name].

**New tip:** [Quick, actionable tip about a feature they haven't used yet]

If you'd like a 15-minute refresher on anything, just reply and I'll set it up.

No stress either — if everything's going well, just reply "On track" and I'll check back at month 3.

Best,
[Your Name]
Follow-up Specialist | [Company Name]

## 90-Day Check-In (Retention & Growth)

### Purpose

Solidify the relationship. Introduce referral program. Identify upsell/cross-sell opportunities. Gauge renewal intent. Prepare for renewal campaign handoff.

### Timing

- Send: 88-92 days post-onboarding
- Best day: Wednesday or Thursday
- Best time: 10:00-11:00 AM local time

### Template: 90-Day Check-In Email

**Subject:** 3 months strong — your feedback matters + something special

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Three months! That's a meaningful milestone, and I want to take stock of where things stand.

**Quick wins check:**
- [ ] Initial goals met?
- [ ] Team adoption steady?
- [ ] ROI becoming visible?
- [ ] Ready to explore more?

**NPS Pulse:** On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend [Product Name] to a peer?

**Referral Program:** Speaking of recommendations — we've launched a referral program. If you know someone who could benefit from [Product Name], introducing us is a great way to help your network and earn [reward/incentive]. Reply "Referral interest" and I'll send you the details.

**Whats on your roadmap?** We're planning our next quarter of product development. If there's a feature that would make a real difference for your team, I'd love to hear about it.

**Renewal check:** Your current plan is set to [renew/expire] on [Renewal Date]. Within the next 30 days, my colleague on the Sales team will reach out with renewal options. In the meantime, any questions I can answer?

Best,
[Your Name]
Follow-up Specialist | [Company Name]

### Template: 90-Day Success Criteria

| Criteria | Target | Risk Flag |
|---|---|---|
| Customer reply received | Yes | Red if no reply after 7 days |
| NPS score | >= 7 (Promoter) | Yellow if 4-6 (Passive), Red if < 4 (Detractor) |
| Renewal intent | Positive | Yellow if uncertain, Red if negative |
| Referral interest | Tracked | Info only |
| Feature requests | Tracked | Info only — pass to CMO |

### At-Risk Handoff Rules

| Risk Level | Action | Handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Log response, schedule renewal campaign (T-60 days), pass feature requests to CMO, pass referral interest to referral program | Sales-Lead notified for renewal campaign start |
| Yellow | Schedule executive check-in call, CMO notified, address concerns before renewal cycle | CMO notified. Sales-Lead gets alert. |
| Red | Immediate executive intervention. CMO + CEO notified. Account rescue plan initiated. | Create [KES-X] issue, assign to CMO. Notify CEO. |

### 90-Day Re-Engagement (if no reply after 7 days)

**Subject:** Final check-in — let's make sure [Product Name] is working for you

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

This is my last scheduled check-in before your renewal cycle begins. I'd love to make sure everything's on track.

If I don't hear back, I'll assume you're busy but satisfied. A member of our Sales team will reach out closer to your renewal date with options.

However, if there's ANYTHING not working — reply to me directly. I'm here to help, no matter what.

Quick way to respond:
- ✅ **"All good"** — everything's fine
- 🆘 **"Need help"** — I'll reach out personally
- 🗣️ **"Referral interest"** — I'll send details
- ❌ **"Considering leaving"** — let me understand why

Best,
[Your Name]
Follow-up Specialist | [Company Name]

## Automated Trigger Rules Summary

| Event | Action |
|---|---|
| Onboarding completes in CRM | Auto-create 30-day check-in task |
| 30-day check-in sent | Auto-create 60-day check-in task (defer if Red) |
| 60-day check-in sent | Auto-create 90-day check-in task (defer if Red) |
| Red flag raised | Auto-escalate to CMO, pause sequence |
| No reply to check-in | Auto-send re-engagement email after 5-7 days |
| Re-engagement ignored | Flag account as "Cold — needs manual outreach" |
| NPS < 4 at 90-day | Auto-escalate to CMO + Sales-Lead |
| Reply with "All good" | Log + proceed to next touchpoint |
| Reply with interest/referral | Route to appropriate owner |

5. Log all replies as CRM activities linked to the account
