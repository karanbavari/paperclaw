Getting Started

BoldGrowth is an all-in-one growth platform that combines real-time analytics, AI-powered automation, and seamless integrations. It helps modern teams accelerate growth by providing actionable insights, eliminating repetitive tasks, and connecting their entire tech stack.
Signing up is easy! Visit our signup page and create a free account with your work email. You will get access to a 14-day free trial with full platform features — no credit card required. Our quickstart guide will walk you through the setup process step by step.
Yes! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. You get full access to all features, including premium integrations and automation workflows. No credit card is required to start. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time during or after the trial.

Pricing & Billing

Our pricing starts at $29/month for the Starter plan (up to 5 team members). The Growth plan at $79/month is our most popular option for scaling teams. Enterprise plans are custom-priced based on your organization's needs. Visit our pricing page for full details.
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and you only pay the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Yes! Annual billing saves you up to 20% compared to monthly billing. The discount is automatically applied when you choose annual billing during checkout. Enterprise customers can also negotiate custom contract terms for multi-year commitments.
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and wire transfers for annual Enterprise plans. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Integrations

BoldGrowth supports 200+ integrations with popular tools including Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira, Notion, Google Analytics, GitHub, and many more. Our integration marketplace is updated weekly with new connectors. Visit our integrations page for the complete list.
Most integrations can be set up in under 15 minutes. Simply navigate to the Integrations section, search for your tool, and click Connect. OAuth-based integrations require just a few clicks. For custom API integrations, our documentation provides step-by-step guidance.

Security & Compliance

Yes. BoldGrowth is SOC 2 Type II certified. We undergo regular third-party audits to ensure our security controls meet the highest standards. Our compliance framework covers security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.
All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We implement strict access controls, multi-factor authentication, and comprehensive audit logging. Your data is stored in secure, redundant data centers with 99.9% uptime SLA.
