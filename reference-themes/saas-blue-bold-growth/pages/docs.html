Quickstart Guide

Welcome to BoldGrowth! This guide will walk you through setting up your account, connecting your first tool, and exploring your dashboard in under 15 minutes.

1. Create Your Account

Sign up for a free BoldGrowth account at boldgrowth.com/signup. You will need your work email to get started. No credit card is required for the 14-day trial.

2. Connect Your First Integration

BoldGrowth connects with 200+ tools. To add your first integration:

  • Navigate to Settings > Integrations from the sidebar
  • Search for the tool you want to connect (e.g., Slack, HubSpot, Jira)
  • Click Connect and follow the OAuth flow
  • Configure data sync preferences and field mappings

Data will begin syncing within minutes. You can monitor sync status from the Integrations dashboard.

3. Set Up Your First Dashboard

Create a custom analytics dashboard to track what matters most to your team:

  • Go to Analytics > Dashboards and click New Dashboard
  • Choose from pre-built templates or start from scratch
  • Drag and drop widgets: line charts, bar graphs, number cards, tables
  • Apply filters by date range, team, or data source
  • Share the dashboard with your team or export as PDF

4. Create Your First Automation

Automate repetitive tasks with BoldGrowth's workflow engine:

  • Navigate to Automation > Workflows
  • Click Create Workflow and choose a trigger event
  • Add conditions and actions using the visual builder
  • Test your workflow with sample data
  • Enable the workflow and monitor its performance

5. Invite Your Team

Collaborate with your team in shared workspaces:

  • Go to Settings > Team
  • Click Invite Members and enter email addresses
  • Assign roles: Admin, Editor, or Viewer
  • Set permissions per workspace and data source

API Example

Here is how you can track a custom event using the BoldGrowth API:

curl -X POST https://api.boldgrowth.com/v1/events \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "event": "user_signup",
    "properties": {
      "plan": "growth",
      "source": "website"
    },
    "user_id": "usr_12345"
  }'

Check the full API reference for all available endpoints and parameters.
