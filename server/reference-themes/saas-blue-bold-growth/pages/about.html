About Us

Our mission is bold growth

We believe every team deserves the tools to scale without limits. BoldGrowth was built to make that belief a reality.

BoldGrowth was founded in 2020 by a team of engineers and product leaders who were frustrated with the complexity of existing growth platforms. We saw teams spending more time configuring tools than actually growing their business.

Our vision was simple: build a platform that combines powerful analytics, intelligent automation, and seamless integrations into one cohesive experience. A platform that works out of the box but scales with you as your needs evolve.

Today, BoldGrowth powers over 10,000 teams across 50 countries. From fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, our customers rely on us to accelerate their growth every single day. We have grown to 200+ team members across offices in Bangalore, San Francisco, and London.

But our mission remains the same: empower every team to grow boldly, without limits.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped us

2020

BoldGrowth is founded

Anika and Rajesh start BoldGrowth from a small office in Bangalore with a vision to simplify growth tools.

2021

First 1,000 customers

BoldGrowth reaches 1,000 active teams. Raises $5M seed round led by Accel Partners. Team grows to 25.

2022

Platform expansion

Launches AI automation engine and integration marketplace with 100+ connectors. Series A raises $25M.

2023

Enterprise-ready launch

Introduces SOC 2 Type II compliance, SSO, white-label options, and advanced analytics. Office opens in San Francisco.

2024

10K teams milestone

Surpasses 10,000 active teams. 200+ employees across three continents. Series B raises $75M. London office opens.

2025

Global expansion

Launches in 10 new languages. Reaches 50+ country presence. Named in Forbes Cloud 100. Continues rapid innovation.
